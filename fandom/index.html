The Understanding
of Fandom

덕질의 이해  ·  A monodrama for your fandom

70 min  ·  1 performer  ·  Ages 6+

Synopsis

Professor Kim Jeong-soon teaches Korean traditional music at university — and never stops fangirling. When her daughter suddenly drops out and returns home without a word, the family's worry collides with Jeong-soon's all-consuming passion. Through ticketing battles, fan sign queues, and a last-chance concert, one question rises to the surface: is your fandom truly understood?
